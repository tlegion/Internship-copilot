# Comparison guide

For each job, report its title, company, location and Job ID. Compare:

- Explicit technologies and responsibilities.
- Stated experience, qualifications and other requirements.
- Details matching the user's expressed preferences.
- Missing information that affects the decision.

Label interpretations as interpretations. A technology mentioned in duties is not
automatically a mandatory prerequisite. Retrieval rank is relevance to the query,
not a measure of the user's suitability. Preserve meaningful differences rather
than filling gaps with assumptions.
